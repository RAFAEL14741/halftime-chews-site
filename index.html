Questions.

Which strength should I get?

Start on green. Over an hour, hot out, or salt marks on your shirt: each yes moves you up one color.

How many do I take?

2 chews about every 30 minutes of hard play or work. One pack of 4 is about 1 hour.

Do I still need water?

Yes. Keep drinking water and eating normal meals. The chews help replace electrolytes lost in sweat.

Who is red for?

Heavy sweaters in the heat, ages 16+ only. Heavy sweaters can lose 950 to 1,900 mg of sodium an hour, so even red only covers part of it.

Who shouldn't take it?

It's for ages 14+ (red is 16+). If you have high blood pressure, ask a doctor first.

Will it melt in my bag?

No. The chews are made to be heat-resistant, so they hold up in a hot bag.

Can my team get its own colors?

Yes. Orders of 10+ boxes get packs printed in your team colors at the same price. Fundraiser boxes let your team keep $5 a box.
